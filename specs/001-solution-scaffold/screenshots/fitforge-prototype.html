04 · Exercise detail

Reference plus your history with this lift. The second half is why members come back.

Barbell Bench Press

Chest · secondary: Triceps, Front delts

Barbell Bench Rack Intermediate

How to

  1. Set the bar at mid-chest height; lie back with eyes under the bar.
  2. Grip just outside shoulder width; wrists stacked over elbows.
  3. Unrack, lower to the sternum with elbows ~45° from the torso.
  4. Press back to lockout without flaring the elbows.

Cues

  • · Shoulder blades down and back, glued to the bench.
  • · Bar path is a shallow arc, not a straight line.
  • · Feet drive into the floor throughout.

Annotations — H1 Logged sets are read-only rows with a success check; only the current row has inputs. Warm-up sets show "W" instead of a number and are excluded from every total (logic §4). H2 Inputs are 40px tall with 16px text — never smaller; this screen is used with sweaty thumbs. H3 "Log set" persists immediately; there is no save-workout step.
